I-Heart Alert ixhuma ebhandeni lakho lesifuba lokushaya kwenhliziyo le-Bluetooth, ebhandeni lengalo noma ewashini futhi ikhale ngaso leso sikhathi lapho ukushaya kwenhliziyo kwenyuka ngaphezulu noma kwehla ngaphansi kwebanga olibekile. Beka umkhawulo ophansi nophezulu, ubeke ifoni eceleni, uzivocavoce ngendlebe.

Akudingeki ubuke isikrini. Azikho izibalo zezindawo. Alikho i-akhawunti.

<h2>Isebenza kanjani</h2>
1. Gqoka ibhande lakho lesifuba noma lengalo, noma uvule ukusakaza kokushaya kwenhliziyo ewashini lakho.
2. Beka i-BPM ephansi nephezulu.
3. Cindezela qala — uthola isexwayiso lapho wenyuka kakhulu noma wehla kakhulu, futhi esinye lapho usubuyele ebangeni.

<h2>Izici</h2>
– Imikhawulo ephansi nephezulu nge-BPM, njengoba ufisa
– Izexwayiso zokushaya okuphezulu nokuphansi
– Izexwayiso zomsindo ngokushesha, ivolumu iyalungiseka
– Ukudlidliza uma ukufuna
– Ukuphinda njalo ngemizuzwana engu-1, 3, 5 noma 10 uma usaphume ebangeni
– Ukubambezeleka kwe-"usuphume ebangeni isikhathi esingaka", ukuze ukuqhuma okufushane noma ukwehla kwesikhashana kuhlale kuthule
– Ukubambezeleka kokuqala, ukuze ukufudumeza kudlule ngokuthula
– Izinga lebhethri lenzwa ngokubuka kanye
– Iqhubeka ngemuva ngenkathi usebenzisa enye i-app
– Ukusetha okusheshayo, akukho okumele ubhalisele

<h2>Isebenza nebhande lakho lesifuba</h2>
I-Heart Alert yakhelwe amabhande esifuba okushaya kwenhliziyo e-Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), okubandakanya:
– Polar H10, Polar H9, Polar H7
– Garmin HRM 600, HRM 200, HRM-Pro Plus, HRM-Pro, HRM-Fit, HRM-Dual
– Wahoo TRACKR Heart Rate, TICKR, TICKR X
– CooSpo H6, H808S, H9Z
– Magene H64, H303
– Suunto Smart Heart Rate Belt
– Decathlon Kiprun / Kalenji Dual HRM belt
– iGPSPORT HR40, Moofit, XOSS, Bryton, Sigma namanye amabhande esifuba e-BLE avamile

<h2>Namabhande engalo namawashi futhi</h2>
– Amabhande engalo: Polar Verity Sense, Polar OH1, Scosche Rhythm, Wahoo TICKR FIT, CooSpo HW807
– Amawashi asakaza ukushaya kwenhliziyo nge-Bluetooth: Garmin (Sakaza ukushaya kwenhliziyo), Polar (yabelana namanye amadivayisi), Coros, Suunto, Amazfit, Huawei, Xiaomi Smart Band, Whoop

Ngaphambi kokusesha, vula ukusakaza ewashini lakho. I-Apple Watch, amawashi amaningi e-Wear OS ne-Fitbit awakusakazi, ngakho awakwazi ukuxhuma.

Noma iyiphi idivayisi ethumela isignali evamile yokushaya kwenhliziyo ye-Bluetooth LE kufanele isebenze, nakuba i-Bluetooth yodwa ingaqinisekisi ukuhambisana, futhi izinzwa ezine-ANT+ kuphela ngeke zixhume. Uma eyakho ingatholakali, thumela umyalezo kumthuthukisi.

Kudingeka inzwa yangaphandle yokushaya kwenhliziyo. Ukukala ngekhamera akusekelwe.

<h2>Yenzelwe</h2>
– Indawo 2 nokwakha isisekelo se-aerobic
– Ukugijima, ukugibela ibhayisikili nebhayisikili elingaphakathi
– I-cardio, i-gym nokuqeqeshwa kwezikhathi
– Ukubekezela nomsebenzi wesivinini esilinganayo
– Izinsuku ezilula zokululama, lapho inhloso kuwukuthi ungasheshisi
– Noma ubani ofuna i-alamu yokushaya kwenhliziyo, angafuni okunye

<h2>Kungani i-alamu esikhundleni sesikrini?</h2>
Inombolo esikrinini isiza kuphela uma uyibuka. Isexwayiso sona sinakekela ibanga lakho: ucabanga ngalo kuphela lapho kudingeka ngempela — ku-treadmill, emgwaqweni, noma ifoni isephaketheni.

<h2>Intengo</h2>
Izikhathi ezi-5 zokuqala zimahhala. Ukulandelela okungenamkhawulo kuwukuthenga kanye. Akukho okubhaliselwa, azikho izikhangiso.

<h2>Ubumfihlo</h2>
I-Heart Alert ayinayo nhlobo imvume ye-inthanethi. Ukushaya kwenhliziyo yakho akuze kuphume efonini yakho.

<h2>Kubalulekile: ukusebenzisa ngemuva</h2>

Akulona ithuluzi lezokwelapha, alihloselwe ukuxilonga, ukwelapha noma ukuvimbela noma yisiphi isifo, futhi akufanele lithenjwe ekuqapheni okuphuthumayo noma okuqhubekayo kwezempilo. Uma unemibuzo ngenhliziyo yakho noma ngokuqeqeshwa kwakho, khuluma nodokotela.
